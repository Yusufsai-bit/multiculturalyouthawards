# Project Architecture Rules

- Keep winner QR codes as local static assets mapped by normalised winner name, because they originate from the official annual booklet rather than editable database records.

## Italic accent words in headings
Gold accent words inside all-caps Playfair Display headings must carry `normal-case` and Title case (e.g. "Champions", "Excellence") — Playfair Display Italic's current Google Fonts build draws capital "N" with a swash tail that reads like an apostrophe ("CHAMPION'S"). Why: avoids a typo-like artifact on every italic uppercase N.
